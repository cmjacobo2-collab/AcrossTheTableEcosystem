Connie Ramirez is the founder of Across the Table, a Phoenix-based business technology and education company focused on helping entrepreneurs and small-business owners build stronger, more sustainable businesses.

She holds a Bachelor of Science in Finance from Arizona State University and an MBA from the University of Phoenix. With approximately 20 years of experience in information technology, systems analysis, application management, and business operations, Connie combines financial knowledge, strategic thinking, and technology expertise.

As a solo founder, she is developing an interconnected ecosystem of business applications and educational frameworks supporting entrepreneurs from business concept through execution, leadership, marketing, operations, and growth.

Her mission is to make business strategy, practical education, and technology more accessible to entrepreneurs and small-business owners.

The company

About Across the Table

Across the Table is a business technology and education company developing an integrated ecosystem of applications, frameworks, and resources to help entrepreneurs and small-business owners start, manage, and grow their businesses.

Its vision is to connect business planning, strategic leadership, execution, marketing, customer engagement, and business intelligence within one accessible ecosystem.
