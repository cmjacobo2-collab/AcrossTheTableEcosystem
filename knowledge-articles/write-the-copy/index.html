The job these three do

You need words constantly. Website pages, emails to your list, social posts, the description of a service you have explained a hundred times out loud but never written down.

Writing them takes longer than it should, so they do not get written, so your marketing stays thin.

These three generate marketing copy. Before you read further, know that a general assistant like ChatGPT also writes copy, free. These three earn their price only in specific situations, and the article is honest about which.

Who each one is

Jasper — the brand one. Brand-trained marketing copy across campaigns and channels. Best for Marketing teams keeping one voice across a lot of output. Watch out: Needs brand training to beat a general assistant.

Jasper is trained on your brand so everything it writes sounds the same. That only matters if several people are producing marketing and it currently sounds like several people.

Copy.ai — the sales one. Go-to-market copy and workflow automation. Best for Sales and marketing content at volume. Watch out: Quality varies by template.

Copy.ai is built around sales and marketing processes rather than one-off pieces — sequences, outreach, repeatable campaigns.

Writesonic — the search one. SEO-leaning content generation and optimization. Best for Blog and landing-page programs. Watch out: Edit hard — SEO output reads like SEO output.

Writesonic leans toward content meant to be found in search. Useful if you are running a blog to attract customers, irrelevant if you are not.

Be honest about the first row. If brand consistency across a team is not your problem, search traffic is not your plan, and you have no outbound process, none of these beats a free general assistant.

How a business actually uses each one

A business with three people writing. Everything used to sound like whoever wrote it. Jasper trained on the brand means the website, the emails and the posts sound like one company.

A company doing outbound. Copy.ai builds the sequence rather than the single email — first touch, follow-up, final note — so a process exists rather than a one-off.

A business betting on search. Writesonic drafts the blog posts. The hard rule: edit heavily. Search-optimised output reads like search-optimised output, and customers can tell.

What it costs

Copy.ai and Writesonic have free tiers. Jasper is paid only, and it is the most expensive of the three.

The honest comparison is not between these three. It is between any of them and the free assistant you may already be using. Try the job in ChatGPT first. If the result is good enough, you have saved yourself a subscription.

Where this sits in your stack

Your marketing AI can become part of a much larger marketing engine. Store approved company and brand information in your knowledge system. Jasper, Copy.ai or Writesonic helps produce the marketing content. Canva creates the visual campaign. Webflow can become the website publishing destination. HubSpot manages the leads generated by the campaign. And automation connects the steps. Now AI content isn’t the end product — it’s one component of a customer-acquisition system.

Canva — creative production
HubSpot — marketing and customer management
Webflow — website publishing
Google Drive — brand and company information
Zapier / Make — automation

The CEO decision

Is your problem that marketing does not get written, or that it reads inconsistently? Only the second one needs these tools.
How many people write marketing in your business? If the answer is one, brand training is solving a problem you do not have.
Have you tried the job in a free assistant first?
Who edits what comes out? Unedited AI copy is recognisable, and customers notice.

Apply it to your business

Take the last piece of marketing you wrote and the last one you meant to write and never did. Give the second one to a free assistant and see what comes back.

If it is usable with editing, your problem was starting, not quality, and you do not need to buy anything. If it comes back generic in a way you cannot fix, that is when a brand-trained tool earns its money.
