Program terms

Last updated November 6, 2026. Offered by Bold and Beautiful Beauties d/b/a Across the Table ("Across the Table," "I," "me").

What you get

  • Free access for six months to the Across the Table apps you choose when you sign up. You can choose as many as you like. TableTap and the SOS (SOSign) apps are not part of this program.
  • Your six months start the day you sign up. Apps marked "Coming soon" are added to your account the day they launch, through the end of your six months.
  • No credit card or payment information is needed to join.

Who can join, and when

  • Signups are open until December 31, 2026 at 11:59 pm Arizona time. There's no limit on the number of Founding Members.
  • One membership per person and email address. You must be 18 or older and joining for a business or a business idea.

What I ask in return

  • A monthly check-in. Once a month I'll email you a short form asking how your business or idea has moved. Honest answers, including what isn't working, are exactly what I want.
  • A follow-up interview. I'll invite you to a short conversation (about 20 minutes) around month three and near the end.
  • If check-ins stop for a long stretch, I may reach out to see if the program is still a fit for you.

Reviews

After your first 30 days, I'll invite you to share an honest review on Google or the Better Business Bureau. This is a request, not a requirement. Your free access never depends on whether you leave a review or on what it says. Please write only about your own real experience.

Your story on my website

  • If you give permission, I may feature your quotes, your name, and your business name on acrossthetable.biz. I'll always use your words as you wrote or said them, and I may shorten them without changing their meaning.
  • Every Founding Member testimonial is clearly labeled as coming from someone who received free access through this program.
  • You can ask me to remove your story at any time by emailing info@acrossthetable.biz.

When your six months end

  • I'll email you two weeks before your free access ends.
  • When it ends, free access stops on its own. You are never charged automatically.
  • To keep using an app, you can sign up at its regular price on its page. Your data stays in your account as described in each app's terms.
  • If you bought an app separately during the program, your paid access is not affected.

Other things to know

  • Each app's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy apply while you use it.
  • The apps are tools to help you run your business. They are not legal, tax, or financial advice.
  • I may change or end this program for future signups. If I need to end it early for current Founding Members, I'll give at least 30 days' notice by email.
  • I use the information you give me only to run this program, improve my apps, and stay in touch about them. I never sell it. See the Privacy Policy.
  • You can leave the program at any time by replying to any program email.
