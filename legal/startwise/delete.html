You can ask us to delete your StartWise account and everything in it at any time. It's free, and you don't need to give a reason. This page explains how, what gets removed, and what we have to keep.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Cancel your membership first if you pay for StartWise, so you are not charged again. Just say so in the same email below and we'll cancel it for you before we delete anything.
  2. Email us from the address you sign in with. Send it to info@acrossthetable.biz with the subject "Delete my StartWise account." This button writes the email for you:
    Email a deletion request
  3. We check it's really you. If you write from a different address, we'll send a short note to the address on the account to confirm before we delete anything.
  4. We delete it and tell you. We act on confirmed requests within 30 days, usually much sooner, and email you when it's done.

Uninstalling the app or clearing your browser does not delete your account. Your data stays in your account until you ask us to remove it.

What we delete

  • Your account: your email address, password, and sign-in history.
  • Your app data: your roadmap progress, tasks, business plan drafts, budgets, cost estimates, and notes.
  • Your agreement record and any saved settings.
  • The copy StartWise keeps in your browser is removed when you sign out. You can also clear it by uninstalling the app or clearing your browser data.

Your account and data are removed from our live systems right away. Backup copies expire within 30 days.

What we keep, and why

  • Purchase records — (your email, plan, and payment dates) as required for billing, tax, and legal purposes. We keep these only as long as the law requires.
  • Basic security logs — kept by our hosting and database providers, which expire on their normal schedule.

We never keep your business data after deletion, and we never sell it.

Want only part of your data deleted?

You can delete items yourself inside StartWise at any time. To remove other specific information, or to get a copy of your data before you delete your account, email info@acrossthetable.biz. We respond within 30 days.

Deletion is permanent. Once your account is deleted we can't bring it back. If you want to use StartWise again later, you'll start a new account.
