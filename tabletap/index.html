How it works

Start with Essential. Choose your plan after we meet.

Only Essential is sold on my Stan Store. Engage and Pro are set up for your restaurant after we talk, so you never pay for a plan you don’t need.

1. Start with Essential. Buy TableTap Essential on my Stan Store. It is $14 per table and tells me about your restaurant.
2. We meet. We walk through your room, your menu and what you want to happen after someone eats.
3. You choose your plan. Stay on Essential or move up to Engage or Pro. Fees are based on your number of tables and locations.
4. I send your link. I send you the link for the plan you chose. You only pay for Engage or Pro if you choose one.
