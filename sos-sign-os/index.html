The problem

Nobody has the whole picture.

Today a sign job passes through several sets of hands: the design files, the permit paperwork, the manufacturer quotes and the install schedule. Every handoff means re-typing details, hunting for the latest drawing, and hoping nothing slipped. When the customer calls, nobody has the whole picture.
